About LAVISH

Our Story

Design that feels personal

LAVISH Interior Designers creates luxury residential and commercial interiors in Borivali West and East, Mumbai. We believe a great interior is more than good looking. It should work for the way you live or run your business.

Our team handles everything under one roof, from space planning and lighting to furniture, decor selection and execution. We are also Vastu consultants, so your layout can follow Vastu principles wherever you want it. Clients tell us the process feels smooth, with clear guidance and thoughtful suggestions at every step.

  • Residential and bungalow interiors
  • Offices and commercial spaces
  • Restaurants and hospitality
  • Lighting and decor selection
